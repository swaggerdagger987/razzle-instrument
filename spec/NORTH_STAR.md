# North star

Razzle exists to quantify your gut.

The spine: find the edge by turning championship odds into the specific players, and the prices, that move them most, under the user's own assumptions.

Two primitives, and they stay in the dance. Every player is viewed through what he does to the odds. The odds always decompose back into players and the price you would pay. Most tools say who is good. Razzle says what you should pay.

Mental model: a Bloomberg terminal for fantasy, with comic-book feel. Players are stocks. The metaphor holds through constraint and comparison. The user is the principal. Razzle is the instrument, with conviction and memory, not a calculator that talks.

## The three rooms

Scratchpad is the research workbench, and it is free. Screener across NFL and college, the formula builder, and Lab views (scatter, heat, radar) where the other axis only offers stats that pair. The player sheet is the tear sheet: summary on top, then one layer at a time. Finance language on top, football inputs one click down. The show-your-work section is real math, never metaphor math.

The Wire is the league desk, and it is paid. It is not a betting market. Connect a Sleeper username once. Monte Carlo championship odds run on the real roster and schedule, under the user's formulas. League reporting and a trade simulator show how one trade moves every team's odds.

War Room ships last, and it is paid. One persistent chat. It speaks unprompted only for an urgent, actionable event. Routine news lives on the player sheet. A quiet room is what makes it meaningful when it speaks. A crisis report waits minutes after real news: what happened, waiver replacements on that league's wire, and an honest bench verdict.

## What sits under all of it

Sleeper context is under every room. Not connected, the front door pitches the decision, not a blank canvas. Connected, the canvas is the user's own roster, and championship odds are the hero number. A flag catches the eye and clicks through to the sheet.

Two layers of value. Everyone gets speed: the tedious work done, the sheet more useful than Sleeper's profile. The edge is what you do with the same information: your thesis, your weights, your odds.

Leverage is a rating, not a slogan. Positions are industries with their own optimal capital ratio, estimated from history, not asserted. It ships as predictive only if it beats an offense-only baseline. Otherwise it is descriptive context.

## What this is not

Not a betting product. Not an "AI" product in the copy. Not a group chat of generic takes. Not a copy of razzle-hq. College coverage matters because dynasty rookie drafts matter. Audience is anyone whose attention shifts hard to fantasy in season, not only the dynasty sliver.
