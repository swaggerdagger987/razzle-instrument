# Acceptance

The build passes or it does not ship. One test.

## The two-guts test

Two users in the same league, with opposite theses, must get meaningfully different championship odds, trade targets, and roster flags.

Example: one models the Bengals as a +2σ offense, the other as a −1σ offense.

If they do not, it is a jukebox, not an instrument.

## What that forces

The formula builder is the strings. Every number on the site (odds, leverage, flags, trade values) must visibly move when the user changes a weight or nudges a σ knob. If the numbers do not dance, the instrument is broken.

The adjustment is standard deviations against a multi-year, schedule-adjusted baseline. The slider speaks in plain language. Sigma stays under the hood.

The odds engine runs on the user's formulas, not on consensus. This is core for every user, not a power-user layer. Skeptics are not argued with. They change the inputs.
